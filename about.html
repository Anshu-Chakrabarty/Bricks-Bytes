About Us

The Intersection of Strategy and Code

Headquartered in Tallinn — the world's most advanced digital society — and at work with clients across five continents.

Who we are

Bricks, then bytes.

Bricks & Bytes Consulting brings together learning strategy, technology and content to help organizations build effective learning ecosystems.

We partner with organizations to navigate the evolving world of Learning Management Systems, digital learning and workforce training. From selecting and deploying the right LMS to developing engaging learning content and supporting training delivery, we help transform knowledge into meaningful learning experiences.

Our philosophy

Strong bricks. Scalable bytes.

Bricks

Strategy, people and culture.

We believe great organizations are built on strong “Bricks” — strategy, people and culture — and scaled through “Bytes” — technology, data and digital learning.

Bytes

Technology, data and digital learning.

In a world where remote work and global expansion are the norms, the ability to train, certify, and align a workforce is no longer a luxury — it is the foundation of survival.

The Tallinn origin

Born in the Baltic tech hub.

Headquartered in Tallinn, Estonia — the world's most advanced digital society — Bricks & Bytes Consulting was born from a simple realization: great organizations are built on solid “Bricks” (strategy and culture), but they scale through “Bytes” (technology and data).

Why Estonia shapes the work

What we do

Digital architects, not software vendors.

We are not just software vendors; we are digital architects. We partner with global enterprises to navigate the complex landscape of Learning Management Systems (LMS). From the initial “Blueprint” phase — where we help you select the right platform — to the “Construction” phase of deployment and content management, we ensure your learning ecosystem is seamless, scalable, and secure.

Our global footprint

Five continents. One standard of care.

While our heart is in the Baltic tech hub, our reach is universal. We serve clients across five continents, helping them deploy internal training platforms for employee excellence and external portals for customer enablement.

At Bricks & Bytes, we don't just implement a system; we build a legacy of knowledge.

Vision

To be the global architectural standard for digital knowledge, where every organization possesses the structural integrity to learn, adapt, and lead in a borderless economy.

Mission

At Bricks & Bytes, our mission is to bridge the gap between traditional corporate foundations and the digital future. We provide expert LMS consultancy, precision deployment, and high-impact content engineering to transform human potential into organizational power.
