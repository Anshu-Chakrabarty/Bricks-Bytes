Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

This policy explains how Bricks & Bytes Consulting, headquartered in Tallinn, Estonia (“we”), handles personal data in connection with this website. It is written for business enquiries. It is not a promise about data inside a client’s own learning platform, which is governed by the contract for that engagement.

Who is responsible

Bricks & Bytes Consulting, Tallinn, Estonia, is the controller for personal data you send us through this website or by email. Contact: hello@bricksnbytes.com.

What this website collects

The site does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or a server-side form database. Pages can be read without creating an account.

If you use the enquiry form, your browser prepares a message containing the details you typed:

  • Name, company or organization, work email, and phone or WhatsApp number if you provide one
  • Country
  • The services and training types you select
  • Your description of the requirement
  • Optional answers on learner numbers, your current LMS, and timing

That message stays on your device until you send it from your own email application to hello@bricksnbytes.com. If you close the page without sending the email, we do not receive the enquiry.

If you email or call us directly, we receive whatever you choose to include in that message.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to understand your learning requirement, reply to you, and prepare a relevant conversation about LMS consulting, implementation, content, training delivery, consultancy, or hosting and support. We do not sell personal data. We do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing.

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation applies, the usual lawful bases are steps taken at your request before entering a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry you started. If a later engagement needs a different basis, that will be set out in the contract.

How long we keep it

Once an email reaches us, we keep the correspondence for as long as we need to answer you and keep a proper record of the conversation. If no engagement follows, we aim to delete or archive the thread within 24 months, unless a legal duty requires longer, or you ask us to delete it sooner and we have no obligation to retain it.

Who else sees it

People at Bricks & Bytes who need the enquiry in order to reply. If a project later involves a specialist, we share only what that person needs. Email travels through your email provider and ours. We do not pass enquiry data to advertising networks.

Consultations may be held with people in Tallinn, London or New York time zones. If a reply or a project requires access from outside the European Economic Area, we do that only as needed to handle your enquiry or engagement, with an appropriate safeguard where the law requires one.

Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, erase it, restrict it, object to certain uses, and ask for a copy in a portable format. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Estonia, that authority is the Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon), aki.ee.

To use these rights, email hello@bricksnbytes.com. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you.

Children

This website is aimed at organisations and the people who buy learning services for them. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this website later stores enquiries on a server, or adds analytics, we will update this policy and change the date at the top before that processing starts. The version you are reading matches the site as published: email is sent only when you send it.
