Solutions

The problem, then the learning.

Services describe what we do. Solutions describe the learner problem in front of you — inside the company, or in an external academy for customers and partners.

01

Employee Learning

Training employees across locations and functions. We help large organizations structure, deploy and manage learning programs across employees, functions, locations and organizational levels.

02

New Employee Onboarding

Structured onboarding journeys for new employees. Learning that helps them understand the organization, their role and the way they work — the bricks a growing team needs to stay aligned.

03

Product Training

Training sales, distribution and service teams. Engaging product learning for employees, distributors, customers and partners so they can communicate value and serve people properly.

04

Technical Training

Complex technical and product knowledge, delivered through structured learning for products, systems, equipment and technical processes.

05

Customer / Partner Training

External academies and customer enablement. Learning experiences that help customers and partners understand products, services, processes and applications — alongside internal platforms for employee excellence.

06

Compliance & Awareness

Mandatory and general awareness training. Learning programs that communicate important policies, standards, processes and organizational expectations, including at enterprise scale.
